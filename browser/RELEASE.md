**Critical Browser** for Windows: a study browser built around Critical.

**To install:** download `Critical-Browser-Setup-….exe` below and run it. Windows may say *"Windows protected your PC"* because the app isn't signed: click **More info → Run anyway**. It installs just for you (no admin password needed) and opens straight away.

- TikTok, YouTube Shorts and social sites are blocked until today's Critical plan is done.
- At plan time it opens itself on your study screen with Critical and Claude side by side.
- Claude panel: Ctrl+Shift+C. Select something and press **Ask Claude** (Ctrl+Shift+A).
- It updates itself from this page.

Only the `.exe` is needed. The other files are for automatic updates.
